Why it Matters

Scientists have proven that shells of marine life today are smaller than during the years of the Challenger expedition. This doesn't just apply to animals, coral has also become increasingly bleached and isn't growing as healthily. This affects a lot of reef animals. The shells of some animals like "the Butterflies of the Sea"(parakods) have decreased by more than 75%. Part of the reason is Ocean Accidification. Many animals, such as salmon and bass, depend on these too. If we don't act on this fast, the impact could be felt as high up in the food chain as us. Many seashore communities rely on seafood as a major source of protein and income. Ocean Acidification could increase poverty and destroy the livelihood of thousands.
